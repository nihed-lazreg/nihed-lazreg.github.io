About

I am a telecommunications engineering student at the National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT), after two years of preparatory studies in physics and chemistry at IPEIM. That path gave me a base in signals, networks and embedded systems, which I now apply to machine learning.

My projects range from deep-learning signature verification to network anomaly detection, with a growing focus on EEG signals, medical imaging and brain-computer interfaces. I am motivated by a long-standing wish to contribute AI-driven solutions to human health and well-being.

I am currently an engineering intern at VEGANET and seek to grow as a researcher as well as an engineer.
